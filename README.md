# SIH 26033 – FARM2MARKET


## AIM:
To develop an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs) with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing unnecessary intermediaries, improving farmers' earnings, and minimizing consumer prices through demand forecasting and logistics optimization.

## PROPOSED SOLUTION:

### Step 1:
Develop a centralized digital marketplace connecting farmers, FPOs, retailers, wholesalers, consumers, and institutional buyers.

### Step 2:
Provide farmer registration and product listing facilities, allowing farmers to upload details such as crop name, quantity, quality, location, and expected price.

### Step 3:
Implement a buyer interface that enables consumers and bulk purchasers to browse available agricultural products and place orders directly with farmers.

### Step 4:
Introduce a transparent price discovery mechanism that allows farmers to compare buyer offers and negotiate better prices.

### Step 5:
Develop a collective selling mechanism that combines agricultural produce from multiple small farmers to fulfil large orders.

### Step 6:
Integrate AI-based demand forecasting to analyse historical sales, seasonal trends, regional consumption, and market prices.

### Step 7:
Implement logistics management to connect farmers with transportation providers and suitable collection centres.

### Step 8:
Develop route optimization using geographical data, vehicle capacity, delivery locations, and transportation distances.

### Step 9:
Integrate digital payment facilities, order tracking, transaction records, and delivery status monitoring.

### Step 10:
Deploy the platform initially in selected agricultural regions and gradually expand it based on farmer participation, buyer demand, and system performance.

## TECHNICAL APPROACH:

### Technologies to be used:

1. Frontend: React.js
2. Mobile Application: Flutter
3. Backend: Python and FastAPI
4. Database: PostgreSQL
5. AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn and XGBoost
6. Route Optimization: Google OR-Tools
7. Mapping: OpenStreetMap
8. Cloud Hosting: AWS / Microsoft Azure
9. Authentication: OTP and JWT
10. Digital Payments: UPI-integrated payment gateway

### Methodology and Implementation:

The system consists of four major layers:

1. User Layer – Farmer, FPO, consumer, buyer and transporter interfaces.
2. Application Layer – Product listings, marketplace, order management and payment processing.
3. Intelligence Layer – Demand forecasting, price analysis and route optimization.
4. Infrastructure Layer – Database, APIs, cloud hosting and security.

### Working Process:

Farmer Registration → Product Listing → AI Demand Analysis → Buyer Matching → Order Placement → Logistics Optimization → Delivery → Digital Payment.

## FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY:

### Analysis of Feasibility:

The proposed system is technically feasible because it uses existing web technologies, cloud infrastructure, machine-learning algorithms, and geographical mapping services.

The initial prototype can be developed with limited infrastructure by integrating existing transportation providers and agricultural collection centres.

### Potential Challenges and Risks:

1. Limited digital literacy among farmers.
2. Inconsistent agricultural product quality.
3. Transportation and storage limitations.
4. Unpredictable agricultural demand.
5. Seasonal price fluctuations.
6. Difficulty in onboarding farmers and buyers.

### Strategies for Overcoming Challenges:

1. Provide multilingual interfaces and voice-assisted operations.
2. Introduce standardized agricultural quality grading.
3. Collaborate with local transport providers and FPOs.
4. Improve forecasting using historical and real-time market data.
5. Display transparent prices and multiple buyer offers.
6. Conduct pilot programmes through farmer organizations.

## IMPACT AND BENEFITS:

### Potential Impact:

The proposed platform primarily targets small and marginal farmers, FPOs, consumers, retailers, wholesalers, and agricultural logistics providers.

### Benefits:

1. Economic Benefits:
   - Improved price realization for farmers.
   - Reduced unnecessary intermediary margins.
   - Better access to agricultural markets.
   - Improved procurement efficiency.

2. Social Benefits:
   - Promotes digital inclusion in rural communities.
   - Encourages farmer participation in digital markets.
   - Supports rural entrepreneurship.
   - Improves transparency in agricultural trading.

3. Environmental Benefits:
   - Reduces unnecessary transportation.
   - Improves vehicle utilization.
   - Helps minimize agricultural wastage.
   - Supports efficient resource utilization.

### Performance Indicators:

1. Average farmer earnings per unit sold.
2. Consumer purchasing price.
3. Transportation cost per kilogram.
4. Percentage of on-time deliveries.
5. Reduction in agricultural produce wastage.
6. Number of active farmers and buyers.
7. Accuracy of AI demand forecasting.
